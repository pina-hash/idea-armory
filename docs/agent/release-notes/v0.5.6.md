## What's new in Armory 0.5.6

Computers on 0.4.1 or later update to 0.5.6 by themselves. Computers still on 0.3.1, 0.3.2 or 0.3.3 need `IDEAArmory-win-Setup.exe` run once, from this release; it keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in.

**A shorter first lesson.** The first-run lesson is now 8 short cards on a practice part: check it out, open it, change it, save, check it in. The control to press is lit, and Next wakes when the step is done.

**First steps on Home.** The same job on your real part, ticked off as you do it. I'm stuck opens Report a problem. Settings, Show me again brings it back.

**Six words, explained where you meet them.** Project, Check out, Changed, Check in, Available and Version each have one sentence and an icon, the same words the website uses.

**Plain help instead of errors.** Technical messages now say what happened and the one thing to do. A refused SolidWorks save reads "You have not checked out this file. Click Check out, then save again in SolidWorks."

**Quieter save warnings.** A burst of blocked saves now shows one notice, not one per attempt.
