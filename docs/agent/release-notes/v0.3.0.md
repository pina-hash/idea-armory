## What's new in Armory 0.3.0

Install 0.3.0 on every computer. Installing it over 0.2.1 keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in.

**Changes show up right away.** When someone checks out a file, checks one in or adds a new one, your computer hears about it the moment it happens, usually within a second or two. Armory still checks on its own every few seconds too, so nothing is missed if the connection hiccups.

**Send feedback.** There is a new Send feedback button at the top of the window, next to Settings (and one in Settings too). Pick Bug, Idea or Other, write what you think, and press Send. Your words go straight to the IDEA team with Armory's version and what it was doing, never what is in your files. Report a problem in Settings still works the same way, and reports Armory saves by itself now reach the team too instead of waiting on your computer.

**Checking out or checking in a whole folder is faster.** Armory now asks for all the files at once. If some of them can't be checked out, the message says which ones and why, and the rest are still checked out for you.

**Force check in for site admins.** The Force check in button now shows for exactly the people ideabosco.com allows: mentors, CAD leads, and site admins.

**When a project is deleted forever.** If a site admin deletes a project forever on ideabosco.com, Armory takes it off your computer by itself and tells you in one line. If a file from it is open, Armory waits until you close it. If you are only removed from a project, nothing changes on your computer: its folder stays where it is, and Armory stops syncing it.

**Your team can see you're online.** While Armory is open, the project's Team view on ideabosco.com shows your computer as "Armory open", and says when it was last heard from after you close it.
