## What's new in Armory 0.5.3

Computers on 0.4.1 or later update to 0.5.3 by themselves. Computers still on 0.3.1, 0.3.2 or 0.3.3 need `IDEAArmory-win-Setup.exe` run once, from this release; it keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in.

**Armory asks the website much less often.** The lab's Armory apps were most of what the website's database answered, and the website students save their work on shares that database. Now Armory looks for your team's changes every 2 minutes while its window is closed or Windows is locked, every 30 seconds while the window is open, and every 10 seconds only while your own files are moving. Opening the window, a click, a save or unlocking Windows still looks at once. When the website is down, Armory waits a little longer each time instead of asking every 10 seconds.

**No more red card about other people's files.** On a shared computer, the team's files in projects you are not in were called "outside every project", with a red card asking you to move hundreds of them. They are not yours, so Armory says nothing about them. If you save a file in a project you are not in, one amber card says so: save it in your own folder.

**Work Armory kept aside is yours to find.** A file you saved in a project you are not in was moved to a hidden folder with no way back from the app. Now the next time you use Armory, a card says your file is safe, and **Show me** opens the folder it is in. Move it into your own folder and Armory uploads it.

**Switching students is faster.** When the last student has nothing left to send, Armory no longer spends up to 15 seconds on one last look before the next student starts, and a look at which files SolidWorks has open can no longer hold everything up for minutes after a sign-in.

**Fewer false alarms.** A file Armory can't make read-only while SolidWorks has it open is a quiet "waiting" note, not a red error. The website being down is no longer reported as Armory crashing.

**Thumbnails.** File pictures still may not show. This version writes down why in its log, so the next one can fix it.

**Also in this release, from 0.5.2 (never published on its own): your own folder in a class project.** When your teacher links an Armory project to your class, you get a folder of your own named with your last and first name, like Reyes_Ana. Save your work there. If you try to save into a classmate's folder or another team's folder, Armory says so plainly, for example "This is Ana's folder. Save your work in your own folder.", and your file stays on your computer until you move it into your own folder.
