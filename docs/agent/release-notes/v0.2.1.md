## What's new in Armory 0.2.1

Install 0.2.1 on every computer. Installing it over 0.2.0 keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in.

**Armory no longer closes by itself during a big download.** On a computer signing in for the first time with a large project, 0.2.0 could close without a word every minute or two and start over. That is fixed. If Armory ever does stop unexpectedly, the next start writes a line about it in its log, so your CAD lead can see where it stopped.

**Buttons answer right away.** When you press Check out, Check in, Undo check out, Open or any other button, it shows a small spinner at once, the line at the bottom of the window says what Armory is doing ("Checking out Bracket.SLDPRT..."), and the file's row says "Checking out...". You no longer wait behind a big download: your check out or check in goes first, and the download carries on after it.

**Changes reach other computers in seconds.** Armory now looks for your team's changes every 2 seconds while you work (every 10 seconds when things are quiet), and keeps looking even in the middle of a long download. A file someone checks out shows as checked out on your computer within a few seconds.

**Open opens the file, even when SolidWorks isn't running yet.** In 0.2.0, Open often said "Wait a moment" and did nothing if SolidWorks was closed. Now it starts SolidWorks the same way a double-click in File Explorer does. If the file isn't on your computer yet, Open downloads it first and opens it when it arrives.

**A check out can't get stuck any more.** If a file is checked out by you, Armory always shows it as "Checked out by you" with a working Check in and Undo check out, wherever the file is and even if it hasn't downloaded yet. Renaming or moving a folder while files are still downloading no longer sends some of them back to the old folder.

**Mentors and CAD leads: Force check in.** "Take back" is now called Force check in. It is on the file's row (for a file someone else has checked out), on the file's page, in the selection bar, and as Force check in all on a folder. Before anything happens, Armory asks you and says who has the files. Anything they hadn't checked in is kept as their own copy, so nothing is lost.

**Report a problem, and problems reported for you.** Settings has a new Report a problem button: pick Bug, Idea or Other, write what happened, and send. When something goes wrong by itself (Armory stops unexpectedly, a button takes more than 10 seconds, the same file keeps failing, a check out had to be repaired), Armory saves a small report of what it was doing at that moment, with file names but never the files themselves. Until ideabosco.com is ready to receive them, reports stay on your computer; Settings has an Open incidents folder link so you can hand them to your CAD lead.
