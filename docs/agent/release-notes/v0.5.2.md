## What's new in Armory 0.5.2

Computers on 0.4.1 or later update to 0.5.2 by themselves. Computers still on 0.3.1, 0.3.2 or 0.3.3 need `IDEAArmory-win-Setup.exe` run once, from this release; it keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in.

**Your own folder in a class project.** When your teacher links an Armory project to your class, you get a folder of your own named with your last and first name, like Reyes_Ana, with a short note inside that keeps it in place. Save your work there. If you try to save into a classmate's folder or another team's folder, Armory now says so plainly, for example "This is Ana's folder. Save your work in your own folder.", and your file stays on your computer until you move it into your own folder. Everyone in the class can still open every file to look.
