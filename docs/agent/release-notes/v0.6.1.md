## What's new in Armory 0.6.1

**No more false "could not reach the server" reports after sleep.** The lab computers sleep and wake often, and for a few seconds after each wake the network is not back yet. Armory now tries again every few seconds for two minutes after a wake, quietly. It reports an outage only when the server stays out of reach for three minutes while the computer is awake and on a network. The check for a new Armory version waits out a wake the same way.

**The cleanup of older Armory copies finishes on shared computers.** Two parts of Armory could write the cleanup's log at the same moment, and that stopped the cleanup. They now take turns. Windows' own programs are no longer listed as Armory copies. If the cleanup still cannot finish, Armory says so once for this version, not at every start.

**Why some computers stayed on 0.3.x.** Where that cleanup always stopped, an old Armory kept starting at sign-in, and the newer Armory handed off to it every time, so neither updated. From this version on the cleanup finishes and the old copy is retired. A computer already stuck that way needs the setup below once.

**See a bigger picture.** On a file's page, click its picture to see it bigger. Press Escape or **Close** to go back.

**More ways to sort.** **Sort files** now also has **Name (Z to A)** and **File type** (parts, then assemblies, then drawings, then the rest). Your sort stays yours on a shared computer, like Tiles.

**Clearer messages.** When the website turns down a Move to Trash or a new shared folder, Armory says why in plain words, never the website's own codes.

**For Mr. Pina:** A computer that still shows 0.3.1, 0.3.2 or 0.3.3 cannot update itself, and nothing can reach it from here. Run **IDEAArmory-win-Setup.exe** from this release once on it (DESKTOP-QH30N35 is the one still reporting 0.3.2), signed in to the Windows account that uses Armory. Do not uninstall first.
