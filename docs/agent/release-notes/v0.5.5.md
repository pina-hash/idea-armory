## What's new in Armory 0.5.5

Computers on 0.4.1 or later update to 0.5.5 by themselves. Computers still on 0.3.1, 0.3.2 or 0.3.3 need `IDEAArmory-win-Setup.exe` run once, from this release; it keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in.

**A first-run lesson.** The first time a student opens Armory, a 19-step lesson walks through projects, folders, checking a file out and in, search and filters, and Report a problem, using a practice file that never touches team files and never opens SolidWorks. Skip it any time; Settings, Getting started, Show me again replays it.

**Back and forward.** Mouse buttons 4 and 5, and Alt+Left and Alt+Right, move back and forward through projects, folders and files, the way they do in Chrome.

**Pictures for lab files.** STL, DXF, G-code (.gcode, .nc, .tap), LightBurn and SVG files now show a picture of the part or toolpath. STEP files show a labeled tile with the part name and size.

**One Armory per computer.** When Armory starts or updates, it finds and retires any older copy on the computer (its shortcuts, its start-at-sign-in entry, its uninstaller), and keeps your files, team folder and settings. A taskbar pin now always opens the same Armory.

**When SolidWorks cannot save.** If SolidWorks tries to save a file Armory is protecting, Armory now says which file and why (you have not checked it out, it is another student's work, or it is in a project you are not on), with one button: Check out, or Save a copy in your folder.
