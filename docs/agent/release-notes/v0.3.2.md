## What's new in Armory 0.3.2

Install 0.3.2 on every computer. Installing it over 0.3.1 keeps your Armory folder, your files and your sign-in. This update comes from the feedback and reports you sent from the Armory itself. Thank you.

**Everything responds right away.** Armory used to spend about 40 seconds of every sync asking Windows, one file at a time, whether each file was open. On a computer with 1,500 files, every click (Check out, Check in, Open) waited behind that. Now Armory asks once for all of them, so a sync with nothing to do takes a second or two and your clicks answer right away. Quitting Armory is quick again too, so Windows no longer has to force it closed (which showed up as a crash report).

**Check in all and Undo all, at the top of My files.** When you have a lot of files checked out, My files now has Check in all and Undo all keys at the top, and its list scrolls in a box of its own, so Team files and its keys stay right under it instead of below thousands of rows. Undo all asks first. In a big folder, the folder's keys stay at the top while you scroll.

**See what Armory is doing.** During a long check out, check in or download, Right now shows a few lines of what Armory just did, the newest at the bottom: "Getting 1,400 files ready to check out", "Checked out 500 of 1,400 files", "Downloaded Plate.SLDPRT (612 KB)". You can see it's working and not stuck.

**Pictures of your parts.** Parts, assemblies and drawings show the same picture File Explorer shows (when SolidWorks is installed on the computer), in each file's row and bigger on its page. Files without a picture keep their usual icon.

**Send feedback says when it was sent.** Feedback that went through sometimes said "Armory couldn't send that feedback." It was sent. Now it says "Sent. Thank you for the feedback."

**Themes switch instantly.** Picking IDEA or Space White in Settings changes the window at once, with no flicker back to the old theme.

**Taking turns on a lab computer.** When another student signs in to Armory on a computer whose Armory folder is yours, they can press Use this folder. If everything of yours is saved to Armory (nothing checked out, nothing waiting to upload, no new files that aren't in Armory yet), the folder becomes theirs and nothing of yours changes. If something of yours is still waiting, Armory says what it is, and you can sign in to finish it. There is also a new Switch account key under This computer, which signs you out and starts the next student's sign-in in one step.
